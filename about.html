About ARIES

A research hub built on evidence, not assumption

ARIES is the research hub of Amaze Consortium, dedicated to conducting structured grassroots research across Bangladesh and beyond, focused on understanding real-world data across multiple fields and domains.

It aims to move beyond assumptions and surface-level interpretations by gathering direct insights from individuals, communities, and institutions. Through systematic data collection and analysis, ARIES explores patterns in behaviour, perception, systems, and societal gaps that are often overlooked in conventional studies.

ARIES is designed to function as a bridge between raw information and practical action. Its goal is not only to study problems but to transform findings into meaningful, evidence-based initiatives that can be implemented within and beyond the Consortium — combining qualitative and quantitative approaches so that insights reflect real conditions rather than theoretical assumptions.

It also serves a second, deliberate purpose. Building and running each study is how ARIES itself grows: every pilot forms the foundation of a standing Amaze Researcher Team, and every finding is designed to feed directly back into an insight-to-action model — where a gap such as low critical-thinking scores among students becomes the brief for a concrete Amaze initiative, like a workshop or essay competition, rather than a statistic that sits on a shelf.

What we stand for

Principles shared across Amaze Consortium, and carried into every ARIES study

01

Community & social impact

Contributing meaningfully to society through responsible initiatives and collaboration.

02

Growth through learning & execution

Gaining real-world experience and developing into capable, disciplined, responsible researchers.

03

Innovation & multi-sector development

Supporting innovation and the growth of diverse fields through a sustainable research ecosystem.

04

Discipline & professionalism

Maintaining professionalism, accountability, and organisational discipline in every study.

05

Leadership & responsibility

Promoting initiative and dependable leadership by encouraging ownership of every role.

06

Collaboration & unity

Uniting purpose-driven people through teamwork, mutual respect, and shared goals.

07

Integrity & transparency

Operating with honesty and fairness in decision-making, communication, and conduct.

08

Long-term impact

Focusing on sustainable growth and lasting impact in Bangladesh and, where possible, globally.
