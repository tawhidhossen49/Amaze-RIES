How to join ARIES

Membership is earned through contribution, not titles or conventional credentials. New members start in a small working group where skills, experience, and commitment are assessed on real work.

Eligibility

Who can apply

✓ Aged between 15 and 30, in accordance with legal requirements
✓ A Bangladeshi citizen
✓ Proficient in both English and Bangla
✓ Polite and formal, with vulgarity entirely absent from conduct
✓ Able to maintain good manners, professionalism, and cultural etiquette

How it works

From new member to research leader

Every new member and associate is placed into a Sub Team — a small, goal-oriented group of about five people with a clear objective and deadline — to build real experience while their skills and commitment are assessed.

01

Join a Sub Team

Placed alongside roughly four peers under an Adjudicator (director) and one or two Vindicators (managers), as an Aide carrying out assigned tasks.

02

Build a track record

Contribution is tracked transparently through a credit system tied to task completion, roles, and participation — visible to the whole organisation.

03

Train through Programs

Short, structured courses — video lessons, classes, assignments, and exams — prepare members for executive roles and recognise Program Graduates.

04

Grow into leadership

Consistent contribution opens the way to executive and director roles, decided through transparent, merit-based governance rather than seniority or age.

The research track

Fellowship & Training Program

Alongside the general Sub Team path, ARIES runs a practical entry point into the Amaze R&D team specifically. Fellows contribute to real research tasks — the pilot survey now in development is a live example — and are assigned into the research team structure based on their performance and engagement.

✓ Work on live tasks: survey design, data collection, and analysis
✓ Performance-based placement into the research team structure
✓ A direct route toward roles like Data Analyst, Methodologist, or Ethics Officer
