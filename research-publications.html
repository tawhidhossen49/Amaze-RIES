Publications

Formal research output from ARIES studies, plus the literature this pilot is grounded in. ARIES has no output of its own yet — Phase 1 hasn’t launched — so this page opens with what the proposal is building on instead of inventing findings that don’t exist.

ARIES’s own findings will appear here once Phase 1 (the pilot survey) has run and been analysed. Nothing is published yet — this section will hold real titles, authors, dates, and abstracts, not sample entries.

Grounding literature

What this pilot is built on

2014

Historical baseline: Khan et al.

Khan et al. — cited in Journal of Education and Social Sciences, Vol. 4

The starting point for this review’s literature scan — focused on building baseline infrastructure and universal enrollment, against which the more recent shift toward skills mismatch and equity gaps is measured.

External source

2026

Bangladesh’s new government sets pathway for education reform

British Council Opportunities Insight

Policy background on the current national reform pathway that frames the environment this pilot is being run in.

External source

2026

Bangladesh must align education with decent work through coordinated reform

Centre for Policy Dialogue (CPD) — Policy Brief

Directly informs the pilot’s job-oriented-mindset strand, framing the gap between graduate output and formal employment this survey is designed to probe.

External source

2024

Quality of primary education in a sub-district of Bangladesh

Directorate of Primary Education (DPE) & UNICEF — International Journal of Humanities Social Sciences and Education

A situational assessment of primary-level quality that informs the pilot’s perceptions-of-education strand.

External source

2024

Strategic Plan for Higher Education (SPHE) in Bangladesh: 2018–2030

Ministry of Education Bangladesh — University Grants Commission

The governing framework for tertiary reform, cited here as structural context rather than as data this pilot collects itself.

External source

2026

Bangladesh’s education system in 2026: between progress and pressure

Rahman, G. — Medium Academic Review

A contemporary synthesis of the same tension — progress against pressure — that this pilot’s survey design responds to directly.
